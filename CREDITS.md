# Credits

## Switch recordings

Every sound in `public/sfx` comes from [kbsim](https://github.com/tplai/kbsim) by
Thomas Lai, used under the MIT license. The files were trimmed of leading silence
and level matched across sets so switching boards does not jump in volume. No
other processing. The full license text is in `public/sfx/LICENSE.txt`.

Sets included: Alpaca, Black Ink, Blue Alps, Box Navy, Buckling Spring, Cream,
Holy Panda, MX Black, MX Blue, MX Brown, Red Ink, Topre, Turquoise.

## Everything else

Layouts, board data, the renderer and the interface are original work for this
project, MIT licensed.
